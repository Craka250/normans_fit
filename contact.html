08 / FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know before starting your journey with us.

01 Do I need to be experienced to join?

Not at all. Our programs are designed for different fitness levels, from beginners to experienced athletes. Our coaches can help you choose the right starting point.

02 Do you offer personal training?

Yes. Our Personal Training program provides individual coaching, personalized workouts and focused guidance based on your goals.

03 What should I bring to the gym?

Bring comfortable workout clothing, suitable training shoes, a water bottle and a towel. Most training equipment is available at the gym.

04 Do you offer CrossFit training?

Yes. Our CrossFit program combines strength, conditioning, endurance and functional movements to provide challenging full-body workouts.

05 How can I become a member?

You can start by contacting our team through the form above or selecting the membership option on our website. We'll guide you through the available membership plans.

06 What membership plans do you offer?

We offer different membership options designed to suit different training needs and fitness goals. You can review our available plans and choose the option that best fits your training journey.

07 Can I visit the gym before becoming a member?

Yes. If you would like to learn more about VYRON FITNESS before joining, you can contact our team to arrange a visit and get more information about our facilities, programs and membership options.
