01 / PRIVACY

PRIVACY POLICY.

Your information matters. This policy explains how VYRON Fitness collects, uses, protects and manages information when you use our website, membership services and digital member experience.

Effective: 22 August 2026 Privacy & Data

01

Overview

VYRON Fitness respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you interact with VYRON, including when you browse our website, create an account, contact us, access member features or otherwise use our services.

By using the VYRON website or creating a VYRON account, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with VYRON, we may collect information such as:

  • Identity information: first name and last name.
  • Contact information: email address and telephone number.
  • Account information: login credentials and information associated with your VYRON member account.
  • Communication information: information you provide when sending an enquiry or message through our contact forms.
  • Membership information: information necessary to manage your relationship with VYRON and your selected fitness services.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated through your browser or device when accessing the website.

VYRON only asks for information that is reasonably relevant to the feature or service being provided.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • create and manage your VYRON account;
  • authenticate your account and maintain your member session;
  • respond to enquiries and messages;
  • provide information about programs, training and membership services;
  • maintain and improve the website;
  • protect the security and integrity of VYRON systems;
  • identify and address technical or security problems; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.
04

Storage & Security

VYRON takes reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.

Some account and session functionality in the current VYRON website uses browser storage. This may include information required to recognize an authenticated member session and support account functionality.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Accordingly, while reasonable safeguards are used, VYRON cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored through electronic systems.

05

Information Sharing

VYRON does not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to operate the service, provide a requested feature, protect VYRON or its users, investigate misuse, or comply with a legal requirement.

Where third-party service providers are used, VYRON expects them to handle information only for legitimate service-related purposes and with appropriate safeguards.

06

Cookies & Local Storage

VYRON may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage or similar mechanisms to support website functionality.

In particular, the member authentication experience may use browser storage for account and session-related information.

Disabling browser storage or blocking certain technologies may prevent some VYRON features from working correctly.

07

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to:

  • request access to personal information held about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion where legally applicable;
  • object to or request restriction of certain processing activities; and
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests should contain enough information for VYRON to understand and respond to the request.

08

Children's Privacy

VYRON services are intended to be used by individuals who are legally able to enter into the relevant membership or service arrangements.

Where a particular program permits participation by a minor, any required parental or guardian consent and applicable safeguarding procedures will apply.

09

Changes to This Policy

VYRON may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal obligations or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised effective date.

10

Contact VYRON

If you have a question, concern or request regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact VYRON through our official contact page.
